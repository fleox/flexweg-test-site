    Claire Martin

    A former physiotherapist, Claire discovered yoga in 2008 to relieve a back worn out by handball. Trained in hatha in Rishikesh and in therapeutic yoga in Paris, she opened Studio Prana in 2016. Her classes are precise, reassuring and adapted to each person's limits.
