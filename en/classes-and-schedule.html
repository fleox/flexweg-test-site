    The classes

    Six ways to practise

    Every class ends with a guided relaxation.

    self_improvement

    Hatha

    Postures held for several breaths to work on alignment, strength and flexibility.

    accessibility_new

    Gentle hatha

    Ideal to get moving again, with back pain or after 60.

    air

    Vinyasa flow

    Dynamic sequences linked to the breath, inspired by ashtanga.

    timer

    Express vinyasa

    One energising lunchtime hour, shower included, back at work refreshed.

    spa

    Yin

    Floor postures held 3 to 5 minutes; Tibetan singing bowl sound bath on Sunday evenings.

    pregnant_woman

    Prenatal yoga

    From the 4th month, with your midwife's approval, in a group of eight mothers-to-be.
