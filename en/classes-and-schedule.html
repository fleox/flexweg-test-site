    The classes

    Hatha yoga

    The heart of our teaching. Postures are held for several breaths to work on alignment, strength and flexibility, and every class ends with a guided relaxation. Gentle hatha is ideal to get moving again or if you have back pain.

    Vinyasa flow

    Dynamic sequences linked to the breath, inspired by ashtanga. You sweat a little and focus a lot. The lunchtime express vinyasa fits into one hour, shower included.

    Yin yoga

    Floor postures held for three to five minutes to release deep tissue and calm the nervous system. On Sunday evenings the session ends with a Tibetan singing bowl sound bath.

    Prenatal yoga

    From the 4th month of pregnancy, with your midwife's approval. Breathing, opening the pelvis, back relief and birth preparation, in a small group of eight mothers-to-be.
