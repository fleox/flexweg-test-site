You don't need an hour of practice every morning to feel the benefits. These three rituals take **fifteen minutes** and can change the way you start your day.

## 1. A glass of warm water and five breaths

Before coffee and before your phone, drink a large glass of warm water, perhaps with a squeeze of lemon. Then sit on the edge of the bed with your feet flat on the floor and take five slow breaths: breathe in for four counts, out for six. Breathing out longer than you breathe in activates the parasympathetic system, the one that calms you down.

## 2. Six postures to wake up your back

On a mat or a rug, flow through these without rushing:

- **cat-cow**, ten times, following the breath;
- **child's pose**, one minute, forehead down;
- **downward dog**, five breaths, knees bent if needed;
- **low lunge**, on each side;
- **seated twist**, on each side;
- **mountain pose**, standing, eyes closed, thirty seconds.

Eight minutes are enough. The goal is not to push but to bring back mobility where the night has stiffened the body.

## 3. An intention for the day

Finish seated, hands on your knees. Choose a word or a short sentence for the day: "patience", "one thing at a time", "I take time for lunch". It is not a resolution, just a landmark to come back to when the day speeds up.

## What if you really have no time?

Keep at least the first ritual: a glass of water and five breaths take two minutes. Consistency matters more than duration, and a small ritual kept every day is worth more than a long session on Sunday.
